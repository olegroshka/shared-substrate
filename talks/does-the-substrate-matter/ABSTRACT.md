# Does the substrate matter?

*Internal talk — 1h slot, flexible talk/discussion split*

In this session, Oleg shares real-world experience using AI-assisted development to
re-engineer a legacy pricing component under tight delivery constraints. Over two weeks the
approach produced 19 gated increments, delivered business-level parity, and produced a
solution now running in UAT.

Rather than focusing on AI productivity claims, the talk explores the engineering practices
that made the work possible:

1. **Structural decomposition**
2. **Explicit guardrails**
3. **System representation**
4. **Continuous validation**

Oleg will examine recent research suggesting that developers may feel faster while actually
delivering more slowly with AI, and discuss what this experiment supports and where the
evidence remains uncertain.

Expect a candid case study, practical lessons, and a lively discussion on what really
matters in effective human–AI collaboration.
