# Does the substrate matter?

*Internal talk — 1h slot, flexible talk/discussion split*

This session shares real-world experience of using AI-assisted development to re-engineer
a legacy component under tight delivery constraints. Over two weeks the approach produced
19 gated increments, delivered business-level parity, and produced a solution now running
in UAT.

Rather than focusing on AI productivity claims, the talk explores the engineering practices
that made the work possible:

1. **Structural decomposition**
2. **Explicit guardrails**
3. **System representation**
4. **Continuous validation**

The talk also examines recent research suggesting that developers may feel faster while
actually delivering more slowly with AI, and discusses what this experiment supports and
where the evidence remains uncertain.

Expect a candid case study, practical lessons, and a lively discussion on what really
matters in effective human–AI collaboration.
